# MOXY Performance Documentation

📚 **SDS & MSDS for Education and AI Training**

This repository provides structured **Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS)** to make critical safety, chemical, and performance information more **accessible, educational, and AI-ready**.

The goal is to ensure that **students, educators, researchers, and professionals** can easily access reliable safety data directly from their **phones, laptops, or computers** — anytime, anywhere.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Centralized Knowledge Base**
  A growing collection of SDS & MSDS files covering a wide range of materials, substances, and use cases.

- **Education-Focused**
  Content tailored for classrooms, universities, and training programs to **improve safety literacy**.

- **AI Training Ready**
  Data is structured to support **machine learning, natural language processing, and AI-based applications**, enabling smarter safety tools.

- **Accessible Anywhere**
  No need to dig through paperwork — information is available **digitally**, whether you’re on a **phone, laptop, or desktop**.

- **Practical for Everyday Life**
  Helps individuals and organizations **understand chemical risks**, make informed decisions, and stay compliant with safety regulations.

---

## 🚀 Benefits

- **For Students & Educators** → Simplifies learning about safety standards, chemical properties, and real-world applications.
- **For Professionals** → Quick access to trusted data during research, workplace safety checks, or compliance audits.
- **For AI Developers** → Rich dataset to train **AI assistants, safety bots, and decision-support tools**.
- **For Everyone** → Promotes safety awareness, responsible handling, and a healthier environment.

---

## 📥 How to Use

1. Browse the repository for available SDS/MSDS documents.
2. Open and read directly in your browser or download for offline use.
3. Integrate with AI tools or educational platforms for enhanced training.
